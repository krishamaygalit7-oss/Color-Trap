One rule. One second. Go!

Do the opposite.

  1. The controller calls a color, for example RED!
  2. Do not press RED. Tap any other available color.
  3. You have exactly one second to respond.
  4. Correct answers earn points. Wrong colors and timeouts cost one life.
  5. Use buttons or press the matching keyboard number, 1–6.

Quick briefing

How to play

  1. Read or listen to the color the controller announces.
  2. Press a different color. The called color is always wrong.
  3. Make your choice before the one-second timer expires.
  4. Correct answers are worth 10 points. Reach a five-answer streak for +20, and a ten-answer streak for +50.
  5. A wrong color or timeout costs one life. The game ends when you run out.
  6. Use mouse, touchscreen, keyboard number keys, or Tab and Enter.

Easy uses four colors and three lives. Normal uses six colors and three lives. Hard uses six colors and two lives. Hard mode also shows recent calls and moves on faster between turns.
